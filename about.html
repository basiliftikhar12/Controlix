About Us

Who We Are

Instrumentation & Control, Done Right

Controlix deals in instrumentation and control systems for Pakistan's process and manufacturing industries. We supply brand new instruments across the four core measurement disciplines that keep a plant running safely — flow, temperature, pressure, and level.

Beyond standard process instrumentation, we specialize in equipment for hazardous and chemical plant environments, including explosion-proof and hazardous-area certified instruments engineered to meet the safety demands of volatile and high-risk process conditions.

We work directly with our clients to specify the right instrument for the right application — then stand behind it with responsive, direct support.

What We Deal In

Core Instrumentation Categories

Flow

Electromagnetic, ultrasonic and mechanical flow meters for liquids and gases.

Temperature

RTDs, thermocouples and temperature transmitters for wide-range process monitoring.

Pressure

Pressure transmitters and gauges rated for liquid, gas and steam applications.

Level

Ultrasonic, radar and float-based level sensors for tanks, silos and open channels.

Specialized Solutions

Explosion-Proof & Hazardous-Area Instrumentation

For chemical and other high-risk process plants, we supply explosion-proof and hazardous-area certified instruments across our full product range — built to perform reliably in environments where standard equipment falls short.
