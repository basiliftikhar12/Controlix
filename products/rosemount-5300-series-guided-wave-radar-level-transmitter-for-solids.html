Rosemount 5300 Series Guided Wave Radar Level Transmitter for Solids

PKR 150,000

The Rosemount 5300 Series Guided Wave Radar Level Transmitter is a premium level measurement solution designed for accurate and reliable continuous level and interface measurement in liquids, slurries, and solids. Using Time Domain Reflectometry (TDR) technology, it delivers highly accurate measurements that are unaffected by changes in density, temperature, pressure, vapor, foam, or turbulence. The transmitter is ideal for demanding industrial applications including oil & gas, chemical processing, power generation, water treatment, food & beverage, and pharmaceutical industries. Its advanced diagnostics, SIL2 certification, and robust construction make it a trusted solution for critical process control and safety applications.
