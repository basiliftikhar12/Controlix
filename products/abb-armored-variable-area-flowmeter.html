ABB Armored Variable Area Flowmeter

PKR 30,000

The ABB AM54031 is an armored variable area flowmeter designed for reliable industrial measurement of liquid and gas flow rates. Part of ABB's AM54 series, the instrument combines a robust armored construction with a local flow indication system and electronic 4-20mA signal transmission for integration with industrial control and monitoring systems. The unit shown is identified as Model AM54031 and is configured for DN25 process connections. Its nameplate specifies a 12-46VDC supply and 4-20mA output, with an ambient operating temperature range of -20°C to +60°C. The AM54 series is designed for demanding industrial applications where durable construction, local indication, and dependable analog signal transmission are required. Depending on the exact configuration, AM54 instruments can support electronic signal transmission and HART communication, allowing process parameters to be integrated into control systems and configured remotely. The AM54031 is suitable for applications requiring continuous flow measurement of compatible liquids or gases in industrial process installations.
