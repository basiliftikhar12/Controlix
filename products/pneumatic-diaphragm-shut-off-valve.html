Pneumatic Diaphragm Shut-Off Valve

The Advance NC3100-10MCB-Z Pneumatic Diaphragm Shut-Off Valve is a high-performance air-operated valve engineered for precise on/off control of ultrapure water, deionized (DI) water, and corrosive chemical fluids. Manufactured with corrosion-resistant plastic wetted parts, it provides excellent chemical compatibility while preventing fluid contamination. The normally closed (NC) fail-safe design ensures reliable and safe operation in industrial process systems, semiconductor manufacturing, water treatment, and chemical processing applications.
