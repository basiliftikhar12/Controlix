Dissolved Oxygen (DO) Sensor with Digital DO Meter/Transmitter

Contact for Price

This dissolved oxygen (DO) measurement system pairs a titanium alloy-bodied DO electrode with a digital DO meter/transmitter featuring an LCD display and onboard calibration controls (MODE/ESC, CAL, SET, INFO, ENT). The sensor incorporates a built-in NTC 10K-ohm temperature element for automatic temperature compensation and responds to 90% of a step change in dissolved oxygen within 60 seconds. It is commonly used in water treatment, aquaculture, wastewater treatment, and industrial process monitoring applications requiring continuous dissolved oxygen measurement. The electrode's membrane cap is a user-replaceable spare part, and 50ml of electrolyte solution is used per replacement.
