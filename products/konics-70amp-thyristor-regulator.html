KONICS 70Amp Thyristor Regulator

PKR 18,000

The KONICS DigiPower 2000 is a digital thyristor power regulator designed for precise control of electrical power in industrial heating and process applications. This unit is rated at 70A and 380VAC and uses a microprocessor-based control system for accurate and reliable power regulation. The DigiPower 2000 supports phase-angle control and zero-crossing cycle control, allowing the controller to be matched to different heater and load requirements. It can provide precise feedback control of voltage, current, and power using RMS measurement technology. The front panel features a digital display, bar LED indicator, RUN/AUTO/EVT indicators, and dedicated control keys for monitoring and configuration. The controller is suitable for integration with temperature control systems and industrial heating equipment where stable and accurate power regulation is required.
