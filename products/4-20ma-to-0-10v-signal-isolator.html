The AICE TECH Intelligent Signal Isolator is a high-performance industrial signal converter designed to convert a standard 4–20mA current input into a precise Double 0–10V voltage output while providing complete electrical isolation. It ensures accurate signal transmission, protects connected PLCs, DCS systems, and control equipment from electrical interference and ground loops, and offers stable long-term performance in demanding industrial environments. Its compact DIN rail design and high conversion accuracy make it ideal for industrial automation, process control, and instrumentation applications.
