WISE Solid Bar Thermowell

PKR 6,500

The WISE Solid Bar Thermowell is designed to protect RTDs, thermocouples, and temperature sensors from high pressure, high flow velocity, corrosion, and harsh process conditions. Unlike cast or fabricated thermowells, these thermowells are precision machined from a single solid stainless steel bar and deep-drilled internally, providing exceptional mechanical strength, durability, and reliability. They are widely used in oil & gas, chemical plants, power generation, food processing, pharmaceutical industries, and other demanding industrial applications.
