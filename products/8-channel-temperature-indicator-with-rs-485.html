8-Channel Temperature Indicator with RS-485

PKR 7000

The Eurotech UR-TAS is an 8-channel temperature indicating and alarm unit designed to monitor multiple thermocouple inputs simultaneously and drive independent relay outputs per channel. Each of the 8 channels accepts a T/C input and can trigger its own dedicated relay output, with Global Out1/Out2 alarm summary outputs and an acknowledge (AL-ACK) input for alarm handling. RS-485 communication allows integration into supervisory monitoring systems. It's commonly used for multi-zone furnace, oven, and process temperature monitoring where independent per-channel alarm indication and switching is required.
