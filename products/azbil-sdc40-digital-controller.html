Azbil SDC40 Digital Controller

The Azbil SDC40 Digital Controller is an industrial process controller designed for monitoring and controlling process variables such as temperature, pressure, flow, and level. The controller provides multiple input and output connections, including 4-20mA, 1-5V, relay, and voltage pulse interfaces. It also supports RS-485 and RS-232C communication, making it suitable for integration with industrial control and monitoring systems.
