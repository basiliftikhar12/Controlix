Thermohunter SA-80T-4A Infrared Temperature Sensor

The Thermohunter SA-80T-4A is a high-performance non-contact infrared temperature sensor designed for accurate surface temperature measurement in industrial environments. With a measurement range of 0 to 400°C and a standard 4–20mA analog output, it easily integrates with PLCs, DCS, SCADA, and other industrial control systems. Its rugged metal housing, IP67 protection rating, fast 100 ms response time, and compact cylindrical design make it ideal for continuous temperature monitoring in harsh industrial applications.
