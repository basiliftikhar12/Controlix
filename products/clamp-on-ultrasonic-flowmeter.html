Clamp-On Ultrasonic Flowmeter

Contact for Price

The Clamp-On Ultrasonic Flowmeter provides highly accurate and non-invasive liquid flow measurement without cutting or modifying the pipeline. Using advanced ultrasonic transit-time technology, it installs externally on the pipe, eliminating pressure loss and process interruption. The integrated LCD display allows local monitoring, while the 4-20mA analog output enables easy integration with PLC, DCS and SCADA systems. It is ideal for water treatment, HVAC, industrial process control, chemical plants, food & beverage, pharmaceuticals and energy management applications.
