475 HART Field Communicator

PKR 590,000

The Emerson 475 HART Field Communicator is a portable handheld device designed for commissioning, configuration, calibration, and diagnostics of HART-compatible field instruments. It enables technicians to communicate directly with pressure transmitters, temperature transmitters, flowmeters, level transmitters, valve positioners, and other HART-enabled devices without interrupting the process. Featuring a rugged industrial design, color touchscreen, and rechargeable battery, the 475 is an essential tool for field maintenance and process automation.
