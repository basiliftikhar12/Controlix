Hanyoung Nux NX3 Multi-Input PID Temperature Controller

PKR 7500

The Hanyoung Nux NX3 is a multi-input digital PID temperature and process controller supporting RTD, thermocouple, and mV/V sensor inputs alongside RS485 communication. It provides two control outputs (SSR/SCR drive or relay), two alarm outputs, and dual 4-20mA current outputs, making it suitable for precise heating and process control applications across industrial automation, plastics, packaging, and furnace/oven control systems.
