Azbil GTX35F Flange-Mounted Differential Pressure Transmitter

PKR 145,000

The Azbil GTX35F (model GTX35F-AAAADAJ1FAAA-AK1AXAX-N1T1) differential pressure transmitter from Azbil's Advanced Transmitter series, designed to mount directly on a tank-side flange. It measures liquid level, interface level and specific gravity, and transmits a 4-20mA DC analog signal with digital communication for self-diagnosis, range resetting and zero adjustment. It is widely used in oil and gas, petrochemical, chemical, and pulp and paper industries, and in any process requiring reliable tank level measurement with corrosion-resistant wetted materials.
