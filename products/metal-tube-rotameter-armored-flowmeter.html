The Tokyo Keiso Explosion-Proof Turbine Flowmeter is a high-accuracy industrial flow measurement instrument designed for both liquids and gases. Built with a full SS316 wetted body, it provides excellent corrosion resistance and long service life in demanding industrial environments. The integrated explosion-proof position transducer (Model MX-250-E) provides a standard 4-20mA analog output while the local indicator allows on-site flow monitoring. Suitable for chemical plants, oil & gas, petrochemical, water treatment, food processing, and other hazardous-area applications requiring reliable flow measurement.
