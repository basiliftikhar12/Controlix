Azbil SDC-36 PID Controller

PKR 25,000

The Azbil SDC-36 is a high-performance digital PID controller designed for precise temperature and process control in industrial automation applications. Featuring advanced PID algorithms, automatic tuning, and universal input compatibility, the SDC-36 delivers exceptional control accuracy, stability, and reliability. It is widely used in furnaces, ovens, boilers, packaging machines, plastic processing equipment, food processing systems, HVAC applications, and various industrial process control systems where precise regulation is essential.

Key Features

  • High-precision PID control for accurate temperature and process regulation.
  • Auto-Tuning (AT) function automatically determines optimal PID parameters for faster commissioning.
  • Supports multiple sensor inputs including Thermocouples (K, J, T, R, S, E, N, B), RTDs (Pt100), and analog voltage/current signals.
  • Bright and easy-to-read digital LED display with intuitive front-panel operation.
  • Fast sampling and response time for stable and reliable process control.
  • Multiple output options including Relay, SSR Drive, and Current Output depending on the model.
  • Compact DIN panel-mount design suitable for industrial control panels.
  • High reliability and long operational life for continuous industrial environments.
  • Alarm output functions for over-temperature, sensor failure, and process monitoring.
  • Ideal for industrial heating systems, furnaces, ovens, packaging machinery, plastics, food processing, and laboratory equipment.
