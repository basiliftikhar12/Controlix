Submersible Level Transmitter

PKR 65,000

The SZY-136 Submersible Level Transmitter is a high-quality hydrostatic level measuring instrument developed using advanced diffusion silicon pressure sensor and IC SENSORS circuit technology sourced from NOVO Company in the United States, applying silicon fine-etching and silicon wafer lamination processes. It is widely used across petroleum, chemical, metallurgy, environmental protection, food, water conservancy, urban water supply, oil field and other industries requiring precision liquid level detection. Featuring a solid-state structure with no moving parts, the transmitter delivers good stability, high accuracy, and a strong performance-to-price ratio. It is highly resistant to viscous media fouling, electrical fatigue, vibration, and shock, and now supports HART protocol communication alongside its standard 4-20mA output, enabling digital configuration, diagnostics, and remote parameter adjustment over the same two-wire loop. Note: this model is intended for use in open containers only.
