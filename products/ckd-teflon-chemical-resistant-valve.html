The CKD MMD402-20BUA-16 Teflon Chemical-Resistant Valve is designed for industrial applications involving corrosive chemicals and chemically aggressive fluids. Constructed from Teflon (PTFE), it provides chemical resistance for applications where conventional metal valves may be unsuitable. The valve has a main pressure range of 0-0.4 MPa (0-4 bar), as indicated on its product label. It is suitable for chemical processing systems, chemical transfer lines, and industrial fluid-handling applications where material compatibility and corrosion resistance are essential. Its threaded connections support installation in compatible piping systems. Before use, confirm the chemical compatibility, operating temperature, pressure limits, and connection specifications for the intended process.
