KROHNE Rotameter (Armoured Flowmeter)

PKR 25,000

The KROHNE H250 RR M9 HT B ESK EEx is a robust variable area flowmeter designed for industrial flow measurement of liquids and gases. The unit shown features an H250 RR stainless-steel measuring section, M9 standard indicator, HT high-temperature design, B heating-jacket configuration, ESK electronic transmitter, and EEx explosion-protected construction. The front plate identifies the measuring application as Fatty Amines and indicates a process density of approximately 800 kg/m³, process temperature of 230°C, operating pressure of 5.8 barg, and a pressure test value of 29 bar. The rear nameplate identifies the unit as H250 RR M9 HT B ESK EEx, manufactured in 2001, with Ex certification for hazardous-area applications. The integrated ESKII apparatus is specified with Ui 30 V, Ii 100 mA, Pi 1 W, Ci ≤ 20 nF, and Li approximately 0 µH. The H250 M9 series uses a mechanical flow indication principle and can be equipped with electronic signal transmission depending on configuration. KROHNE documentation identifies H250 M9 as a variable area flowmeter for liquids and gases and lists optional electronic outputs including 4-20mA/HART on applicable configurations. The exact measuring range, process connection size, and electrical output configuration should be confirmed from the complete instrument documentation before installation or replacement.
